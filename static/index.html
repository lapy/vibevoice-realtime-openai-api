Post-processing

Speech-friendly defaults: high-pass 60 Hz cuts rumble and sub-bass without touching normal voice fundamentals (typical adult F0 ~80–250 Hz). Low-pass 0 leaves the full band up to Nyquist at 24 kHz—avoid setting it low (<8 kHz) or you will dull consonants and “air”. Edge fade and peak ceiling reduce clicks and digital overs; they do not narrow the vocal formant range.
